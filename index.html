FAQ · Nail salon in Rhodes

Frequently asked questions

Where is MAG Nails in Rhodes?

MAG Nails and Beauty Shop is a nail and beauty salon at Alexandrou Papadiamanti 4 in Rhodes Town, on the island of Rhodes, Greece.

Which nail and beauty services do you offer in Rhodes?

Manicure, gel polish and rubber base manicure, gel and acrygel nail reinforcement, nail art, pedicure and foot treatments, permanent brows (powder brows), semi-permanent lip make-up, lash lift and tint, brow lamination and waxing.

How do I book a nail appointment in Rhodes?

Book online through Treatwell at any time, day or night. You can also call +30 22410 69344 or send a WhatsApp message to +30 6955 36 9825.

Do you do permanent make-up for brows and lips in Rhodes?

Yes. Every permanent brow is designed from scratch to suit your face. We offer powder brows, cover-ups of old brow tattoos and semi-permanent lip blush, with a touch-up after healing.

Do you offer pedicure and treatments for cracked heels?

Yes. Our pedicure treatment removes dry skin and cracked heels and leaves your feet soft and hydrated. We also treat ingrown nails, calluses, corns and thickened nails.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday 09:00 to 20:00, Friday 12:00 to 20:00, Saturday 09:00 to 17:00. Closed on Sunday.
